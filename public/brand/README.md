# The Story Scrolls brand assets

The mark is an unfurling story-scroll drawn as an `S`: a path into a story, a
page being made, and the Story Scrolls initial in one compact shape.

## Files

- `storyscrolls-mark.svg` — scalable master mark for light or dark placement.
- `storyscrolls-social-master.png` — full-resolution social artwork master.
- `/favicon.svg`, `/favicon.ico`, and `/favicon-32x32.png` — browser icons.
- `/apple-touch-icon.png` — Apple home-screen icon.
- `/icon-192.png`, `/icon-512.png`, and `/icon-maskable-512.png` — installable app icons.
- `/og.png` — 1200×630 Open Graph and large Twitter/X card.

## Core palette

- Midnight ink: `#070b11`
- Deep story blue: `#172637`
- Antique gold: `#d4a452`
- Illuminated parchment: `#f3d798`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to roughly one quarter of its width.
Use the full-color mark at 24px or larger; generated favicon variants are
optically checked for smaller placements.
